## What types of breast implants are there?

There are two main [types of breast implants](/implants/) currently available for use – saline and silicone. However there are more than one type of saline and silicone implants. For example [IDEAL implants](/implants/ideal-implants/) are a new type of saline implants that eliminate a lot of the problems with traditional saline implants, such as rippling and folding. They also provide a more natural feel.

As far as silicone implants go, you have the option to get [‘gummy bear’ implants](/implants/gummy-bear-implants/), which are still made from silicone but are more cohesive, meaning they keep their shape better and are safer in the event of a rupture.

## Which is better - saline or silicone?

This is not a simple question to answer since both types of implants have their own advantages and disadvantages. For example, [saline implants](/implants/saline-implants/) are cheaper and can be used for women 18 years old and above. [Silicone implants](/implants/silicone-implants/) provide a more natural feel but they cost more and you must be at least 22 years old to have them.

Your surgeon will be able to best advise you when you have your consultation.

## Are implants safe?

Yes, for the most part implants are very safe. The main issue with implants is ruptures. Ruptures tend to occur after a number of years but can easily be corrected with a new surgery. Ruptures with saline implants are easy to detect but if you get silicone implants then it’s recommended you have an MRI once every three years.

## Which type of implants cost more?

Silicone implants usually cost more than saline. This can vary depending on the type of saline or silicone implants you get however.

## Are implants only used for breast augmentation?

No. Breast implants can also be used in a breast lift or as part of breast reconstruction surgery. The reason implants are most often associated with breast augmentation is simply because that is the most popular form of surgery where they’re used.

## How do I choose the right surgeon for breast augmentation?

Choosing the [right surgeon to perform a breast augmentation](/about/) is extremely important. Some of the key things to look for in a surgeon are –

-   Board certification
-   A lot of experience performing breast augmentation surgery
-   “Before” and “after” pictures of their previous surgeries
