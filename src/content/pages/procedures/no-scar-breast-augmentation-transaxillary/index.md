**Breast augmentation is a form of cosmetic surgery that comes with many different options.** Not only are there different types of implants to choose from but the incisions can be made in different places and the implants can be placed either above or below the chest muscle.

Transaxillary breast augmentation has become increasingly popular in recent years since it enables you to have breast surgery performed without any scars left behind on the actual breasts. any scars left behind on the breasts themselves.

## How transaxillary breast augmentation is performed

Transaxillary breast augmentation is performed by making incisions at the armpits, through which the implants are inserted. The implants can be inserted with great precision through the use of an endoscope – a thin tube that has a camera and light on the end of it.

Once the implants are in place, the incision is then sutured and will heal naturally. Depending on the type of implants used, the patient will need to follow some aftercare advice, such as massaging the new implants to make sure they settle into place properly.

## Is transaxillary breast augmentation safe?

Yes, transaxillary breast augmentation is very safe and carried out at multiple clinics throughout the country. It is growing in popularity all the time thanks to the fact that no scars are left on the breasts.

## Are there any downsides?

Generally the benefits of transaxillary breast augmentation outweigh the downsides, though that isn’t to say there aren’t any. Achieving perfect symmetry can be a little tougher compared to other methods but this usually isn’t an issue when the surgeon performing the procedure has a lot of experience with this particular method.

## What type of implants can be used?

Both saline and silicone implants can be used with transaxillary breast augmentation but the length of the scar will be affected by the type of implants. Saline implants are often used in transaxillary breast augmentation since they can be filled after being inserted and therefore a smaller incision can be made.
