## Preparing for your recovery

Below are some steps to take to ensure that your recovery starts as well as possible –

-   **Make sure your affairs are in order and your schedule is free –** The last thing you want when you’re recovering from breast surgery is to be worrying about other things. You should make sure you schedule is free and that you have at least a week where you can take it easy and are not obligated to do anything strenuous.
-   **Arrange for a ride to and from the hospital –** Driving is absolutely out of the question in the 24 hour period that you have your surgery. Therefore you need to arrange for somebody to take you to and from the hospital.
-   **Make sure you have someone who can spend the first 24 hours with you –** This is very important for your own safety. During the 24 hours following your surgery, you are more prone to falling or fainting so you absolutely need to have someone with you during this time. If you’re unable to find somebody then a support worker can be arranged for an additional fee.
