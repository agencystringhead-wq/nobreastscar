Our signature procedure

The no-scar breast augmentation, explained

Transaxillary augmentation places the implant through a small incision in the armpit, so there are no scars on the breasts themselves.

Read the full guide →
  1. 01
    A small incision in the armpit
    The implant goes in through the armpit, away from the breast, where the incision tucks into a natural fold.
  2. 02
    Guided by an endoscope
    A thin tube with a camera and light on the end lets the surgeon see inside and place each implant precisely.
  3. 03
    Saline or silicone
    Both can be used. Saline implants are often chosen because they're filled after insertion, which allows a smaller incision.
  4. 04
    Closed, then left to heal
    The incision is sutured and heals on its own. Depending on the implant, you may be shown how to massage it so it settles properly.
  5. !
    The honest trade-off
    Perfect symmetry is harder to achieve through the armpit than with other methods. That's why experience matters, and why it's our specialty.

Implant FAQ

Questions women ask us first

Every implant question →
Which is better, saline or silicone?+
Neither is better for everyone. Saline costs less and is available from age 18. Silicone feels more natural, costs more and is available from age 22. We help you choose at your consultation.
Are breast implants safe?+
Implants are generally very safe. The main concern is rupture, which usually happens after several years and can be corrected with surgery. Saline ruptures are easy to spot; MRI checks are recommended for silicone.
What types of implants are there?+
Saline and silicone are the two main types, each with variants such as IDEAL saline implants and more cohesive gummy bear silicone implants.
Which implants cost more?+
Silicone implants usually cost more than saline, though prices vary by the specific implant.
Are implants only used for augmentation?+
No. They are also used in breast lifts and breast reconstruction.
How do I choose the right surgeon?+
Look for board certification, extensive experience with breast augmentation, and before and after photos of previous patients.
